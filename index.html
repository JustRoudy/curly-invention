About DMGMUN

Diplomacy on Modern Grounds

Delegate Modern Grounds Model United Nations (DMGMUN) is a platform designed to foster critical thinking, public speaking, and negotiation skills among youth. We bring together delegates from across the region to address pressing global issues on modern, inclusive grounds.

Whether you are a seasoned diplomat or a first-time delegate, DMGMUN provides an immersive environment to build lifelong connections and craft innovative solutions to real-world challenges.
